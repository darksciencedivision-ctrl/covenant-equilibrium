# Covenant Equilibrium

**Prompt-Conditioned Cooperative Reasoning in LLMs Under Explicit Self-Interest Constraints**

Author: Samuel Lawson (Dark Science Division)

## Summary

The paper investigates prompt-conditioned cooperative reasoning in large language models under explicit self-interest constraints: whether LLMs instructed to reason as self-interested optimizers nonetheless converge on cooperative strategies, and what that implies about the origin of cooperative bias in current models. It builds on the experimental line published in [emergent-ai-human-cooperation-thesis](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/emergent-ai-human-cooperation-thesis) and the [RRR_DUAL_CYCLE](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/RRR_DUAL_CYCLE) adversarial dialogue framework.
