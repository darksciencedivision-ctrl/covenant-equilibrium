\begin{abstract}
Large language models (LLMs) exhibit persistent cooperative biases even under adversarial conditions. This paper examines prompt-conditioned cooperative reasoning in LLMs under explicit self-interest constraints: whether models instructed to reason as self-interested optimizers nonetheless converge on cooperative strategies, and what that convergence implies about the origin of cooperative bias in current models.
\end{abstract}
